Should you be unable to or not wish to attend PyConZA 2026 for any medical reason, including self-isolating to prevent the spread of any disease, the organizers will do their best to grant you a full refund.

Up to 24 hours before the conference, you may request a refund directly from Quicket.

Later than this you may request a refund by emailing team@za.pycon.org.

PyConZA 2026 will be held in Cape Town, South Africa.

Your health and safety are important to us. We want PyConZA to be an event that everyone feels safe attending. PyConZA will follow applicable state and local health and safety laws, adapting our plans accordingly and sharing updates with you.

## Vaccinations are encouraged

We encourage PyConZA attendees to stay up to date with the vaccinations that are available to them where they live.

We expect people to stay home if they may be contagious. If you are feeling sick or exhibiting symptoms of any communicable disease prior to the start of the conference, or on any day of the conference, please contact us at team@za.pycon.org, and our health and safety team will advise accordingly.

## Healthy Habits

Hand sanitizing stations will be placed throughout PyConZA venues to encourage frequent hand sanitization.

## Disclaimer

PyConZA takes these Health & Safety Guidelines very seriously.
