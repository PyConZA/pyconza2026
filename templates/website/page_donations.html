What we built together

PyConZA is more than just a conference. We exist to open doors, connect people, and plant seeds that grow into careers, projects, and stronger communities. As a completely volunteer-run, non-profit event, all money raised goes directly toward making Python more accessible to people who might not otherwise have had the chance to get involved.

Your support helps us welcome more people into our community.

How we work

Since we're completely volunteer-run, donations go directly to participants and programming. Our organizers contribute their time because they believe in what we're building.

We don't have overhead costs or salaries eating into contributions. Donations fund opportunities, experiences, and futures.

Why this matters

Technology is growing rapidly across South Africa, but learning opportunities aren't equally available everywhere. Your support for PyConZA helps remove barriers that prevent talented people from getting involved in tech.

You also helped build bridges between developers who might otherwise work in isolation, and created the kind of catalyst experiences that turn curiosity into careers and careers into community leadership.

The people we supported go on to support others in their own communities. The impact multiplies.
