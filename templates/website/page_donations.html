What we build together

PyConZA is more than a conference. It exists to open doors, connect people, and plant seeds that grow into careers, projects, and stronger communities. The event is volunteer-run and non-profit — money raised goes directly to making Python more accessible for people who would not otherwise have the chance.

Your support helps us welcome more people into the community.

How we work

PyConZA is run by volunteers. Donations go directly to participants and programming. Organisers contribute their time because they believe in what we are building.

No overhead, no salaries — donations fund opportunities, experiences, and futures.

Why this matters

Tech is growing across South Africa, but learning opportunities are not equally available everywhere. Supporting PyConZA helps remove barriers that keep talented people out.

It also builds bridges between developers who would otherwise work in isolation, and creates the kind of catalyst experience that turns curiosity into a career and a career into community leadership.

The people we support go on to support others in their own communities. The impact multiplies.
