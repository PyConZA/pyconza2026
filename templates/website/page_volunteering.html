Volunteer with us

PyConZA is a non-profit event organised by the community for the community. Everything we do is powered by volunteers who care about Python and open source.

Why volunteer?

Volunteering is a good way to meet other Python people from around the country, build lasting connections, and grow your network. You contribute directly to the conference and help create a space where knowledge is shared and ideas circulate.

Connect

Meet people from across the Python community.

Give back

Help create an inclusive and welcoming conference.

Make an impact

Help build the South African Python community.

What you can do

We have a range of roles. Read the role descriptions.

None of the roles need specialised knowledge. We will show you everything.

Beginner's Day mentor

Help newcomers work through the Humble Data workshop on Wednesday 14 October. If you're comfortable with basic Python and you're patient and friendly, you're a great fit.

Registration and help desk

Scan tickets, hand out badges and swag, and answer attendee questions at the registration desk.

Setup

Lay out the registration area, sponsor area, and signage on the morning of the first conference day.

Teardown

Pack up equipment and signage at the end of the last conference day.

Session AV

Monitor AV equipment, point the video camera, manage microphones. No expert knowledge required.

Session chair

Introduce speakers, run Q&A, and keep an eye on the time.

Remote session chair assistant

Watch the Discord channel for a talk and pick good audience questions for the session chair to read out.

Remote help desk and vibe

Hang out on the Discord help-desk channel, answer questions, and welcome people on the other channels.

Social media

Attend talks and workshops and post highlights on your own accounts with the #PyConZA2026 hashtag. We'll boost your posts.
