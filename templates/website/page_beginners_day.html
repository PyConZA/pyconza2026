What is Beginner's Day?

Beginner's Day is a pre-conference event for newcomers to the Python community. Through hands-on workshops and mentorship, we open doors for aspiring developers, data scientists, and technologists from any background.

Our aim

To create a supportive environment where anyone can start their Python journey, whatever their technical background.

Advanced Pythonistas welcome too

We are all beginners at something. Even experienced Python developers will find topics here that are new to them — from data visualisation with Plotnine to game development with PyGame.

Learn something new

Expand your toolkit by exploring areas outside your day-to-day. Data scientists can try game development; web developers can dig into data visualisation; everyone can pick up new libraries and approaches.

Share your knowledge

Join us as a coach. Your experience helps newcomers through their first Python steps. Teaching deepens your own understanding and gives back to the community.

Why Beginner's Day matters

By making Python accessible to more people, we are:

  • • Lowering barriers that keep people out of tech.
  • • Broadening the Python community across South Africa and the wider continent.
  • • Opening pathways for under-represented groups.
  • • Building confidence for people new to technical conferences.
  • • Providing a welcoming entry point to PyConZA itself.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any programming experience?

No. Beginner's Day is designed for people with no prior programming experience.

What if I'm not selected?

We keep a waitlist and notify you if a spot opens. You can also use our online resources and community channels in the meantime.

Can I attend both Beginner's Day and the main conference?

Yes. Main-conference tickets are separate; Beginner's Day attendees get a discount.

What operating system should my laptop have?

Windows, macOS, or Linux all work. We'll help with setup on the day.
