PyConZA 2026 will be available online for attendees anywhere in the world. Join us from wherever you are.

How the online experience works

We do not just stream talks. The online experience is interactive — you can take part in discussions, ask questions, and connect with other attendees.

Conference dates: October 2026 (dates TBD).

What you get: live streams, recordings, and access to the PyConZA Discord community.

How to join the online experience

Step 1: Get an online ticket

Online ticket holders get access to all live streams, recordings, and the Discord community.

Step 2: Join the Discord server

Discord is where the community gathers during the conference — chat about talks, ask questions, and meet other attendees.

Join PyConZA Discord

Step 3: Register with the Discord bot

Online attendees register with our Discord bot to access event channels and take part in discussions.

What you get with an online ticket

All the talks

Watch every session live, or catch up later with recordings. You get access to everything.

Real interaction

Not passive watching. Ask questions during talks, join conversations with other attendees, and start your own discussion groups.

Discord community

On the Discord server you can:

  • Chat in dedicated channels for each talk.
  • Ask speakers questions after their presentations.
  • Start your own discussion groups on topics that interest you.
  • Look at what sponsors are working on (some are hiring).
  • Play games and explore other community spaces.

Watch parties

One of the better parts of the online experience is watching with others. Consider organising a watch party with your local Python group, colleagues, or friends — it's a good way to make the experience more social and have real-time discussions.

A few ideas

  • Get your local meetup group together.
  • Watch with your work team.
  • Set something up at your university.
  • Invite a few friends over.

If you are watching alone, you'll still be part of the active Discord community with hundreds of other online attendees.

What you need

A reasonable internet connection and something to watch on — computer, tablet, or phone all work. You will also want to join Discord for the community parts. We send setup instructions before the event.

After the conference

Your ticket includes ongoing access to the recorded talks and the Discord community, so the connections and learning continue after the live streams end.

Join us online

Whether you are watching from another province or another continent, you'll be a real part of PyConZA. The online experience is built for interaction and community.
