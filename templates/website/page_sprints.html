What are sprints?

Sprints are collaborative coding sessions where developers work together on open-source projects. They are a fixture of PyCon conferences worldwide — bringing together project maintainers, regular contributors, and newcomers to make meaningful progress on the tools the community depends on.

What happens during sprints?

  • • Fix bugs and implement new features.
  • • Improve documentation and write tests.
  • • Review pull requests and onboard new contributors.
  • • Share knowledge and pick up new practices.
  • • Build lasting connections with other developers.

Why join the sprints?

For contributors

  • ✓
    Learn from experienced people: work directly with project maintainers and seasoned developers.
  • ✓
    Make an impact: contribute to projects you use every day.
  • ✓
    Pick up new skills: discover new tools and practices.
  • ✓
    Build your network: connect with developers across Africa and beyond.

For project maintainers

  • ✓
    Make progress quickly: get help with features, fixes, and documentation.
  • ✓
    Grow your community: onboard new contributors face-to-face.
  • ✓
    Get fresh perspective: hear from people outside your usual contributor base.
  • ✓
    Share the workload: build a stronger, more distributed contributor base.
