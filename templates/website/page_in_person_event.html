Why in-person matters

You can watch talks online, but you can't replicate bumping into someone at lunch who is working on the exact problem you're trying to solve. Or the late-night session that starts because someone mentioned an interesting project at the coffee break.

We optimise for connection. Nothing beats the hallway track.

What to expect

Talks from the community

Two days of presentations, from beginner-friendly introductions to advanced deep dives. Speakers come from across Africa and beyond, sharing their experiences, projects, and insights.

Hands-on workshops

Sessions where you can dig deeper into specific topics. Designed for people who want to learn by doing.

Open spaces

Some of the best conversations happen in the unstructured time. Open spaces let you organise discussions on whatever interests you — a specific technology, career advice, or just connecting with people working in similar areas.

The hallway track

Conversations by the coffee machine, impromptu debugging sessions, "you should meet this person" introductions. We schedule plenty of breaks and informal time because this is often the most valuable part of the conference.
