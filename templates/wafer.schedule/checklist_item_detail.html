Preparation Checklist

  • Introduce yourself to the speaker and confirm their talk description and how to pronounce their names
  • Ask if there is anything you need to take note of.
  • Show the speaker cards that indicate how close they are to the end of the talk
  • Make sure the speaker has tested their laptop with the projector (AV team may need to assist)
  • Make sure the speaker is familiar with their microphone (AV team may need to assist)
{% endblock preparation %} {% block talk_start %}

Talk checklist

  • At the start, introduce the speaker and the talk,
  • Let the online attendees know that they can ask questions, but to use !question so that they show up.
  • Ask the audience to give them a round of applause to begin the talk.
{% endblock talk_start %} {% block during_talk %}

During the talk

  • Warn the speaker with the cards when their talk is about to end

The below times are only indicative. We usually have, for example, a 15-minute buffer for a 30-minute talk in a 45-minute schedule slot, so if the speaker starts 10 minutes late, then they still get their full 30 minutes, and 5 minutes of Q&A.

Q&A

When the speaker has finished, and there is time for questions:

  • Thank the speaker and compliment them on the talk, and announce that they are ready for Q&A
  • EVERY audience member who is asking questions is using the handheld microphone (otherwise online viewers won't hear them)
  • Check for questions from online attendees.
  • When there are no more questions, or there is no more time left, thank the speaker again and ask for a final applause
{% endblock qa %} {% block post_talk %}
