Sponsorship of PyConZA will give your organisation broad visibility and recognition. It is an excellent way to support the Python community, promote your products to our hundreds of delegates, and meet Python developers who are looking for work.

Your sponsorship reaches a broad, growing community — across South Africa, Southern Africa, the wider African continent, and beyond:

South Africa

Corporate teams and academics from across the country join us in person, our largest delegate base.

Southern Africa

A special financial assistance programme helps delegates from Southern African communities attend in person.

Africa

Free online tickets shared with other African communities widen our reach and impact across the continent.

Beyond

International guests join us every year, both online and in person.

In return for your support, we will ensure that your company is given exposure before, during, and after the event, and we will work with you so that you get exactly what you're looking for out of sponsoring the conference.
